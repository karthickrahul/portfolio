I'm an industrial engineer who likes taking a part that fails too often, or a process that runs slower than it should, and finding the specific reason why — then redesigning it. That approach led to a pump-shaft geometry redesign that improved hydraulic efficiency by 20% and cut mechanical failures, and an elevator shaft safety-door system built to meet global safety standards.

My background spans prototype testing, root-cause analysis and process improvement using Lean tools, alongside CAD work in Fusion 360, Creo and AutoCAD. I'm currently completing a Master's in Industrial Engineering at VSB – Technical University of Ostrava, and looking for opportunities to apply engineering design and continuous-improvement thinking to reliability and production performance.
